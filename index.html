The gap doesn't announce itself. It just quietly widens.

You said this year would be different. Then the days filled up — replies, logistics, the hundred small things that have to happen for anything to happen. Almost none of it was what you meant to spend your life on.

The distance between what you said mattered and what you actually did is the thing OTIUM was built to close.

The idea

You tell it, once and in depth, what you want your life to actually look like — what's worth your time, what isn't, what you want handled, and what you want to keep for yourself.

From then on it works from that. Every demand on your time gets sorted against it: handled quietly, brought to you, or pushed back. You spend your attention on what you chose. The rest gets done without you.

How it works

You write your own rules.

A long first conversation turns what you care about into something the system can act on. It's yours. You change it whenever you want. Nothing is locked.

It handles the moving parts.

One request — “sort out the trip” — and it coordinates the pieces: the bookings, the calendar, the people, the budget you set. Across services, vendors, and, when they're ready, robots.

You see whatever you ask to see.

There's no fixed set of screens. Describe the view you want — this week against your budget, everything you keep putting off — and it builds it. Keep it or drop it.

Once a week, an honest look.

It shows you your life next to what you said you wanted. Not a score. Not advice. Just the truth — so if you're drifting, it's a choice you make on purpose, not one you slide into.

What it will never do

It won't tell you how to live.

There's no built-in idea of a good life in here. Spend your days gaming, or chasing a Nobel — same system, zero judgment. It measures your life against your rules, never anyone else's.

It won't act behind your back.

You hand it control one piece at a time, at your pace. Everything it does can be undone. When it isn't sure, it asks.

It won't lock you in.

The rules you write are yours to take with you. Open standard, open code, your data exportable. If we ever make it worse, someone forks it — and you walk out with everything that matters.

When it's real

You can start today. The full vision — every service open, a robot in every home — is about a decade out.

Right now OTIUM connects your finances, and handles email and phone calls on your behalf. Add a robot and it takes on the physical world too. The rest arrives as daily-life services open up and machines get cheaper and better. You don't wait for that — you grow into it, one piece at a time, at your pace.

Get started

Three steps. In your own time. Stop at any one.

01

Connect your finances

Link your accounts, bills, and budget. It sorts them, catches the duplicate charge, disputes what's wrong, and holds the line on what you set. This is where everyone starts.

02

Let it speak for you

Email and phone calls, handled on your behalf — the renewal, the appointment, the back-and-forth nobody wants. You approve what matters; the rest just gets done.

03

Add a robot

Own or rent a single unit — a Unitree today, more arriving fast. Now the physical world gets handled too: the errands, the chores, the things software alone can't touch.
